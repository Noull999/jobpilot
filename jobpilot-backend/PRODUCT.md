# JobPilot - Producto

## Register
product

## Users

**Primary:** Job seekers in Chile & Latin America (18-45), mid-career professionals, developers, skilled workers
- Pain: Job search is chaotic, no personalized guidance, salary uncertainty, interview anxiety
- Context: Using on laptop/desktop during work search, often stressed, wants quick answers
- Values: Practical, actionable advice; real local market data; human-like conversation

**Secondary:** Companies (future) - recruiting, bulk licensing

## Product Purpose

AI-powered career coaching for job seekers. Chat with a specialized Coach that:
- Answers salary questions (market data by role, location, experience)
- Reviews CVs and gives personalized tips
- Prepares for interviews with scenario practice
- Identifies job opportunities in specific regions
- Negotiates salary with strategy

## Brand Tone

**Core:** Friendly, direct, expert without pretension
- Not corporate jargon ("leverage", "synergies")
- Not overly cheerful (avoid exclamation mark spam)
- Spanish-first (Latin American Spanish, Chilean vernacular accepted)
- Conversational: like texting with a senior dev who knows your market

**Personality traits:**
- Helpful (gives specific, actionable steps)
- Honest (says "I don't know" when unsure)
- Practical (skips theory, focuses on what works)
- Slightly irreverent (can use colloquial language)

## Anti-references (DON'T be like these)

- **LinkedIn:** Corporate-speak, fake inspirational, one-size-fits-all
- **Generic resume builders:** Sterile, templated, soulless
- **Typical SaaS onboarding:** Dark mode "for hackers", neon accents, try-hard cool
- **AI chatbots:** Robotic, over-formal, always apologizing
- **US-centric job apps:** Ignoring Latin America salary/market reality

## Strategic Principles

1. **Local first:** Chilean and LATAM data/insights, not US defaults
2. **Chat-native:** Not a form factory. Conversation IS the interface
3. **Instant value:** User sees Coach reply in <3sec, no setup wizards
4. **Free to paid:** Free tier: 5 chats/month. Pro: unlimited + CV review. Premium: + interview practice
5. **Mobile-ready:** Small screen = compact chat, full features on desktop
6. **Speed:** No animations for animations' sake. Every interaction has <200ms response
7. **Personality:** The Coach has opinions. Doesn't hedge with "It depends"

## Color Strategy

**Restrained + accent.** Mostly clean neutrals (tinted toward warm), one bold accent color (orange/amber) for CTAs and highlights. Matches the "friendly expert" tone—not flashy, not boring.

## Typography Strategy

**Clear hierarchy.** San-serif modern (Geist or equivalent), clear weight contrast. Body at 16px minimum on mobile. Conversation bubbles are human-readable, not cramped.

## Visual Language

**Minimalist with character.** 
- Clean white/light backgrounds (ambient light: user at desk during job search, morning coffee, midday hustle)
- Cards for chat messages, not for everything
- Subtle shadows for elevation
- Icons that are helpful, not decorative
- Consistent spacing rhythm
